Some things in life are more important than code, customer success metrics, or even the perfect dark theme. One of those things is my wonderful wife, Vilde.

Vilde is the kind of person who lights up every room she walks into. She has this incredible ability to make everyone around her feel seen and valued — a skill I try (and often fail) to bring into my customer success work. She's patient when I'm debugging at midnight, supportive when I take on yet another side project, and always ready with a cup of coffee and a smile.

She's my biggest cheerleader, my harshest (but fairest) critic, and my favorite person to share life's adventures with. Whether we're exploring new places, cooking together, or just enjoying a quiet evening at home, everything is better with her.

Vilde, if you're reading this: thank you for being you. You make every day better.
